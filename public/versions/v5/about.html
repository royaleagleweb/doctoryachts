About Doctor Yachts

An independent mechanic practice for boats and yachts in South Florida.

The name is the method. We treat vessels like systems—power, propulsion, pumps, wiring— not a parts catalog with a dock.

We’re not a brokerage, not a detailing crew, and not a full paint-and-refit yard. Engines, electrical, cooling, bilge, and the gray area where those systems fight each other— that’s the work. Free estimates after diagnosis for recommended repairs.

Serving Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, Miami, and nearby docks when access allows.
