# Doctor Yachts — SEO + AEO ranking strategy

Creative angle: **“The boat doctor”** — diagnostic-first marine mechanic for Fort Lauderdale / Pompano / Miami. Own questions AI answers and local “boat repair near me” intent.

## Ranking pillars

| Pillar | Why it ranks | Our pages |
|--------|----------------|-----------|
| **Local service + city** | Drew-style money keywords | `/services/*`, `/locations/*` |
| **Problem guides (AEO)** | Featured snippets + AI citations | `/guides/*` |
| **Entity clarity** | Knowledge graph / AI trust | Schema, `llms.txt`, consistent NAP |
| **Conversion pages** | Capture ready buyers | `/free-estimate`, `/book`, `/contact` |
| **Proof** | Reviews + FAQ depth | `/reviews`, `/faq` |

## SEO best practices (implemented)

1. **One intent per URL** — service silos, city pages, guides  
2. **Title formula** — `Keyword | Doctor Yachts | City, FL`  
3. **Canonical + sitemap + robots** — index money pages; noindex `/versions`  
4. **Internal links** — guides ↔ services ↔ cities ↔ estimate  
5. **Geo copy** — Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, Miami on money pages  
6. **Full HTML pages** — not SPA shells; static generation for crawl speed  

## AEO best practices (implemented)

1. **Direct answer boxes** (~40–60 words) at top of guides + services  
2. **Question-shaped H2s** in guides  
3. **FAQPage schema** on FAQ, guides, services  
4. **HowTo schema** on checklist guides  
5. **Article schema** on guides  
6. **LocalBusiness / Service schema** sitewide  
7. **`llms.txt`** for AI crawlers summarizing the entity  
8. **Visible FAQs** matching schema (no hidden junk)  

## Creative ways to outrank generic shops

1. **Own the “doctor / diagnose first” entity** — unique brand story competitors don’t claim  
2. **Win long-tail questions** before head terms  
   - “boat won’t start checklist”  
   - “why is my boat engine overheating”  
   - “how often service boat Florida”  
3. **Match Drew’s local playbook** (mobile + FTL/Pompano) with better content depth  
4. **Service × city internal linking** (matrix over time)  
5. **Free estimate landing** for “boat repair estimate Fort Lauderdale”  
6. **Publish weekly** one new guide from real customer questions  
7. **Google Business Profile** (must-do offline) matching NAP on site
