## SEO formulas to steal (not wording)

### 1. Title formula
```
[Primary keyword], [Secondary keyword] | [Brand] | [City A] & [City B], FL
```
Example: `Boat Repair, Boat Maintenance | Doctor Yachts | Fort Lauderdale & Miami, FL`

### 2. Meta formula
```
Get expert [service] in [City], FL. We handle [list 2–3 services]. Contact us for a free estimate!
```

### 3. H1 formula
```
[Benefit or urgency] + [Service] + [City A or City B, FL Area]
```
Example: `Don't Wait — Get Boat Repairs in the Fort Lauderdale or Miami, FL Area`

### 4. H2 formula (always geo)
```
[Action] for [service] in [City A], [City B], FL or the surrounding area
```

### 5. Content must-haves (every service page)

- City names **2–4 times** (natural)  
- Phone number mid-page  
- Free estimate / call CTA  
- Inboard **and** outboard when engines  
- Mobile / on-site / dockside language  
- Bullet “why us” (experience, mobile, certified brands)  

### 6. Trust bullets (home + about)

- Free estimates  
- Years of experience  
- Mobile / on-site  
- Brand certifications (Mercury, Yamaha, etc.)
